Class selection

Rules reference

The compendium lists 11 main classes: Genjutsu Specialist, Hunter-Nin, Intelligence Operative, Medical-Nin, Ninjutsu Specialist, Scout-Nin, Taijutsu Specialist, Weapon Specialist, Puppet Master, Cooking-Nin and Science-Nin. Specializations are recorded separately and are not treated as main classes.

The clan catalog includes 44 entries plus Non-Clan, Bloodline Latents and Legacy Content as separate character options. This creator keeps those separate to avoid misclassifying them as clans.
